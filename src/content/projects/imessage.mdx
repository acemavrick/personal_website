## About

I wrote a Jupyter notebook that analyzes a database of iMessage conversations and
surfaces some basic statistics and visuals as a fun side project
I worked on because I wanted to see analytics of my texts with people.

I found a Mac-based [tool](https://github.com/ReagentX/imessage-exporter) online
that exports iMessage history and wrote a script to build an
SQLite database from the exported data. I then made scripts to get some stats 
from it, such as sent vs received ratio, message rate over time, 
characters transferred over time, etc. It revealed some pretty interesting 
insights and trends.

I was considering doing some sentiment analysis and other stuff, but decided to
move on to other projects. Maybe sometime later.
